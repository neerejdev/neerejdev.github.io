Journal Article Forthcoming

Algorithmic Acculturation in Adult Social Care: Migrant Workers, Conversational AI and the Making of Professional English

Dev, N.
Journal of Ethnic and Migration Studies
PDF via CV
Drawing on multi-sited field ethnography with Malayali migrant care workers in the UK, this article investigates how workers deploy conversational generative AI models (ChatGPT and Google Gemini) to overcome the racialized and punitive linguistic audits of institutional adult social care. We develop the concept of algorithmic acculturation to illuminate how digital platforms mediate professional incorporation and labour reproduction.

From Rivermind to Care Homes: Settler Dreams and Britain’s Care Crisis

Dev, N.
Economic and Political Weekly, Vol. 61, No. 11 (DOI: 10.71279/epw.v61i11.44344)
PDF DOI
This study examines how post-Brexit immigration regulations and Britain’s chronic care sector staffing deficit have restructured transnational labor migration from South Asia. It charts the trajectory from agrarian and middle-class habitus in Kerala to the intense corporeal and emotional demands of UK residential care.
Journal Article 2026

Human Voices and Algorithmic Echoes: Resignifying Transfeminine Experiences Through a Hybrid Poetics Framework

Dev, N., & Joy, S.
International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction (DOI: 10.1080/10447318.2026.2615218)
PDF DOI
Investigates the capacity of generative AI (GPT-4o) to represent lived transfeminine realities. Utilizing a three-phase Hybrid Poetics Framework (HPF), the study pairs qualitative focus groups with Turing-style discernment experiments, analyzing whether natural language generation can replicate authentic subcultural affect.
