Core Research Strands

Doctoral Project

Scripting the Settler Dream: Instagram Influencers, Generative AI, and the Negotiation of Malayali Masculinities in UK Adult Social Care

Based at the University of Wolverhampton, this doctoral study interrogates how the post-Brexit restructuring of UK health and social care visas has precipitated a new migration corridor from Kerala, India.

Drawing upon extensive ethnographic fieldwork across UK care facilities and community networks, the project traces how social media influencers curate commodified aspirational imaginaries of British settlement, and how migrant men navigate the sudden transition into the intimate, bodily, and feminized routines of elder care.

Field Ethnography Post-Brexit Migration Caring Masculinities Precarity & Debt
Digital Infrastructures & AI

Algorithmic Acculturation: Conversational AI and Workplace Linguistic Surveillance

Investigates how frontline migrant care workers routinely deploy conversational AI (such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Google Gemini) to craft, translate, and audit daily clinical care logs and handovers.

Under strict audit regimes enforced by the Care Quality Commission (CQC), linguistic imperfections or grammatical hesitations expose non-native English speakers to managerial reprimand. Workers utilize generative models as compensatory sociomaterial infrastructures to sanitize their expressive repertoires into standardized British administrative English—a process I conceptualize as algorithmic acculturation.

Algorithmic Acculturation Conversational AI / LLMs CQC Audit Culture Linguistic Insecurity
Poetics & Critical Subcultural Inquiry

The Hybrid Poetics Framework (HPF) & Queer Resignifications

In collaborative scholarship with Silpa Joy published in the International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction, we formulated the Hybrid Poetics Framework (HPF) to investigate whether algorithmic generative models can authentically encapsulate and echo marginalized transfeminine lived experiences.

Through data poetry and incentivized qualitative discernment protocols, this strand interrogates epistemic limits, affective dissonance, and the ethical responsibilities of natural language processing when interfacing with marginalized communities.
