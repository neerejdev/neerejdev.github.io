Research Profile

I am a doctoral researcher at the University of Wolverhampton examining how technology, mobility, and media infrastructures shape gendered identities and social stratification. My doctoral research draws on field ethnography with Malayali migrant care workers to examine masculinity, settler aspiration and labour precarity in the post-Brexit care economy. A closely related strand investigates how migrant workers use conversational AI, such as ChatGPT and Google Gemini, to negotiate the linguistic and documentary demands of professional care, developing the concept of algorithmic acculturation.

My research works have been supported by awards from the Swedish Research Council, the Swedish Collegium for Advanced Study and SOAS. I have also published in peer-reviewed journals including the International Journal of Human–Computer Interaction and Economic and Political Weekly. Alongside the qualitative core I bring statistical-methods and computational training, which I integrate with interpretive analysis, especially for social media analysis.
